# Bremer’s goggles and the unclaimed package

- **Goggles.** [FBI Vault, Bremer Part 121](https://vault.fbi.gov/barker-karpis-gang/bremer-kidnapping/Bremer%20Kidnapping%20Part%20121), p. 018, records the instructions to count to fifteen and the restart at five. The later [Part 138 trial summary](https://vault.fbi.gov/barker-karpis-gang/bremer-kidnapping/Bremer%20Kidnapping%20Part%20138), p. 5, says ten and contains a release-year error. Both put the goggles in the weeds. The [Part 122 Glynn outline](https://vault.fbi.gov/barker-karpis-gang/bremer-kidnapping/Bremer%20Kidnapping%20Part%20122), p. 059, records the weeds, roughly three inches of snow and the Saint Paul transfer. Part 121’s “Goggles” entry, archive stamp 649, says the goggles would go to the U.S. attorney. Neither text establishes a final exhibit ruling. Separate from the existing Bremer wallpaper, clothing and sound inquiries.
- **Cornmeal box.** Part 121, pp. 043–045, records Arthur Murray’s account of the delivery, his mother’s discovery and George M. Murray’s March 31 forwarding of the note to the state bureau in Saint Paul. The outline transcribes the directions about the marked Chevrolet and records a planned transfer of the box to the U.S. attorney. The man’s identity and the execution of the proposed handoff are open. The cornmeal instructions are separate from the existing five flashes and Shell signs story.
- **Reserve.** Part 121, pp. 040–041, has two accounts of another note delivered in a coffee can on a Hague Avenue porch. Kept out to avoid telling two container stories in one batch. Also omitted the hideout’s broken toilet handle, adjacent to the existing wallpaper case.
