# Brand strategy and SWOT

Saint Paul After Dark is a self-paced digital history tour about how Saint Paul’s underworld protection system worked, who benefited from it, and who bore the costs. The brand should invite readers into a seven-chapter story and give them a clear path from a striking scene to the claims and sources behind it. This is the proposed position for the project’s working preview and eventual public edition.

## Brand platform

| Element | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **Saint Paul After Dark**. Spell out Saint Paul in the title. In search copy, use the common variant “St. Paul” where it reads naturally or appears in a source title. |
| Public descriptor | **A digital history tour of Saint Paul’s underworld, 1900–1938**. Include on the first encounter and anywhere the title appears outside the tour. Keep the longer date range when using Prohibition as a search or editorial hook. |
| Tagline | **Every city has rules. Saint Paul had an arrangement.** Use as the campaign line when the descriptor supplies context. |
| Primary action | **Enter the story**. For external copy, support it with “Read at your own pace” or “Start with the prologue.” |
| Brand promise | Understand the arrangement through the people it affected, and see how the tour reached its conclusions. |
| Mission | Make a complicated part of Saint Paul’s history readable at home, with enough context to examine the evidence and its limits. |
| Character | Atmospheric, plainspoken, curious, and careful with attribution. Let lighting and composition create tension. Let the writing tell the reader what happened, who says so, and where the account is uncertain. |

**Positioning statement for planning**

For Twin Cities history readers and curious visitors who want to understand Saint Paul’s gangster era on their own time, Saint Paul After Dark is a digital history tour that follows a system of protection through seven illustrated chapters. Linked claims, source notes and corrections give readers a way to check what the story says. Its central question is who had leverage and who paid for it.

### Audiences and first encounters

| Priority | Audience and need | Useful first encounter | Intended next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Twin Cities residents, former residents and local-history readers who know the names and want to understand the system | A short local-history feature or an image and question about how the arrangement worked | Enter the story and read the prologue |
| 2 | Remote readers and Saint Paul visitors looking for background before or after a trip | Search result or an article on digital Saint Paul history that says “read at home” | Open the chapter directory or contextual map |
| 3 | Librarians, local-history groups, educators and research-minded readers assessing a resource to share | A chapter with a visible claim and qualification plus a direct Method link | Inspect the sources and decide whether to share the chapter |

These are intended audiences. Interest, search volumes, completion and referral activity have yet to be measured. Educator use is a distribution possibility. The edition’s content note covers extortion, abduction, armed confrontation and institutional corruption. [Edition catalog](../src/data/edition.json)

Use atmosphere as invitation, the arrangement as the story, and sources as proof. Pair generated or interpretive scenes with a visible illustration label and their provenance in the credits. Avoid implying exact historical views, recorded historical speech, verified venue access, a physical itinerary, official affiliation, or an original archive held by the project. Reserve accessibility language for specific features with a known acceptance record.

## SWOT

Items are ordered by impact on the proposed public position within each group.

### Strengths · Internal

1. **Clear editorial point of view.** The arrangement, its consequences and the people harmed hold the seven chapters together. That gives the reader a reason to follow the story beyond a list of famous names. [Edition catalog](../src/data/edition.json)
2. **Traceable sourcing.** The claim ledger, qualifications, corrections and bibliographic downloads give the reader inspectable ways to check the account. [Method](../src/pages/method.astro) · [Project status](../README.md)
3. **A flexible reading format.** The complete text, direct chapter links and optional media let the reader choose pace and depth. [Project status](../README.md)

### Weaknesses · Internal

1. **Preview status constrains distribution.** Edition 0.5.0 is explicitly a preview. Human listening, rights, provenance, scene, geography and editorial obligations affect what can be represented as finished. Real-device accessibility acceptance also remains open. [Edition catalog](../src/data/edition.json) · [Review state](../src/lib/release.ts) · [Verification record](../docs/verification.md)
2. **The map has limited precision.** Three approximate venue positions support geographic context. The other four chapters appear in the directory. A campaign built around maps or venue access would overpromise. [Map copy](../src/pages/map.astro) · [Venue catalog](../src/data/locations.json)
3. **First-glance comprehension is untested.** The title and noir treatment can suggest a photo exhibit, nightlife, a ghost event or a ticketed tour. The descriptor has to work at card size and in text-only placements. [Tour cover](../src/pages/index.astro) · [Exact-name exhibition](https://www.mplsart.com/events/saint-paul-after-dark-a-night-photography-exhibit-or-jared-arvin)

### Opportunities · External

1. **An established discovery category.** Visit Saint Paul already curates virtual experiences and gangster-history resources. Use those category terms in appropriate copy, and consider fit with local-history lists once the edition is accepted. Listings or partnerships have yet to be established. [Virtual experiences directory](https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/things-to-do/virtual-tours/) · [Gangster history feature](https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/blog/gangster-past/)
2. **A companion for existing local history experiences.** Bus, walking and courthouse tours supply natural before-and-after contexts for a chapter share. A source-linked digital chapter can extend the topic for people who are curious once they leave. [CynCity’s tour details](https://www.cyncitytours.com/our-tours) · [Landmark Center’s tour details](https://www.landmarkcenter.org/gangster-tours/)
3. **An entry point for readers who want to discuss evidence.** A single chapter that visibly qualifies a familiar story can travel in a local-history reading list or discussion group. Under the Bluff is a candidate because its text distinguishes the venue’s documented entertainment history from claims about criminal associations. [Chapter catalog](../src/data/stops.json)

### Threats · External

1. **Exact-name collision and overlapping visual vocabulary.** Search results and articles may conflate the tour with the local photography exhibition. Use the full name with its descriptor in titles, cards and listing copy. [Exhibition listing](https://www.mplsart.com/events/saint-paul-after-dark-a-night-photography-exhibit-or-jared-arvin)
2. **Strong established destinations for the same question.** Tour operators and major historical institutions already answer many of the obvious queries. A general “gangster history” pitch may have low visibility. Lead specific shares with the arrangement, a place, or a carefully qualified question. [Visit Saint Paul tour directory](https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/things-to-do/tours/) · [MNopedia topic article](https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/event/gangster-era-st-paul-1900-1936)
3. **The category encourages spectacle.** Ghost tours and reenactments make for easy visual hooks. An isolated noir image can also be mistaken for an archive photo or for evidence of a location. Use illustrated thresholds, maps and records with their captions intact. [Landmark Center’s October 2026 program](https://www.landmarkcenter.org/event/gangster-ghost-tour-2026/) · [Project evidence standards](../docs/evidence-ledger.md)
