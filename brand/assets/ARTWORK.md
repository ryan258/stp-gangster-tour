## ImageGen prompts

### city-at-dusk.jpg

> Use case: ads-marketing and historical-scene. Create an original editorial illustration for Saint Paul After Dark, a source-linked digital public history tour about the early twentieth-century underworld in Saint Paul, Minnesota. Wide landscape 3:2. A generalized downtown river-bluff streetscape circa early 1930s, seen from street level in blue hour after rain. A few lit upper-story windows and a low amber glow along a receding commercial street, masonry blocks and steep grades suggest Saint Paul, with a distant curve of water if it fits naturally. Distinctive quiet civic atmosphere. A sophisticated hand-painted screenprint meets a lithograph, with dry paper grain, clean big value shapes, credible restrained period materials, and charcoal-brown, smoke blue, antique parchment and muted copper tones. Architectural mass at center/right, side margins can crop. Use open readable shadows. No recognizable historical figures, posed people, violence, weapons, modern objects, major recognizable landmark, readable lettering, titles, borders or watermark. This is a clearly interpretive illustration, not faux archival photography.

### reading-the-record.jpg

> Use case: historical-scene. Create an original editorial illustration for Saint Paul After Dark, a source-linked digital public history tour. Landscape 3:2. A tightly framed table in a generalized 1930s clerk's office. Three blank aged document sheets partly unfolded across a dark wood table, small graphite pencil and a simple brass paperclip, amber side lighting and subtle shadows. Strong simple arrangement, focused on what a reader can examine. A sophisticated hand-painted screenprint meets a lithograph, dry paper grain, clean shapes, restrained credible period materials, charcoal-brown, antique parchment, muted copper and hints of smoke blue. Plenty of calm, open shadow detail. No people, fingerprints, insignia, identifiable documents, pseudo-writing or readable text, weapons, logos or watermark. Clearly interpretive, no photograph effect.
