# Visual story artwork

The seven chapter shots, opening backdrop, and three-pose transparent visitor sheet were generated with built-in OpenAI ImageGen. The people, interiors, documents, and other props are interpretive. The media catalog records the WebP digests and keeps provenance and distribution rights pending owner review.

## Motion between poses

`story-flow-*.bin` files hold forward and backward displacement maps for the eight gaps in each nine-pose sheet. The maps use a 40 × 40 grid and load as the corresponding scene comes into view. The browser shifts both adjacent poses toward the exact scroll position and blends their transparent edges. The fingerprint develops in place. The map generator constrains movement around the telephone base and door casing.

The same scroll fraction drives the CSS blend when motion maps or WebGL are unavailable. Calm view, reduced motion, print, and JavaScript-free reading show the completed pose.

The header of [`scripts/generate-story-flow.cpp`](../scripts/generate-story-flow.cpp) records the regeneration commands and binary layout. Regenerate a scene’s map whenever its nine-pose WebP changes.
